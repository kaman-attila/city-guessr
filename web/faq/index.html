Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the daily challenge, scoring, accounts and the data behind the game. Can't find what you are looking for? Write to contact@pinthecity.me.

Playing

Is PinTheCity free?

Yes. Every mode is free to play, with no limits on Freeplay and no paid extras. You do not even need an account.

When does the daily challenge reset?

A new daily challenge starts every day at midnight UTC. Depending on where you live that can be in the evening, at night or in the morning; the menu shows a countdown to the next one.

Does everyone get the same cities?

In the daily challenge, yes: everyone in the world gets the same five cities in the same order, which is what makes comparing scores fair. In Freeplay every game is random.

Can I play the daily challenge more than once?

No, you get one try per day. If you want more practice, Freeplay has no limit. Past challenges you have not played yet are waiting in the daily archive.

I missed a day. Can I still play it?

Yes. Open the daily archive from the menu to see every past challenge. Days you have not played can be played there, and your score is added to that day's results. Days you have finished show your score and the full breakdown again.

Why is the last round so hard?

On purpose. The five rounds go from Easy to Impossible. The first city is usually a capital or a world-famous city, while the fifth is often a small town that even many locals of that country would have to think about. A good score on round five is something to be proud of.

Scoring

How are points calculated?

Each round is worth up to 1000 points. A pin within 10 km of the city gets all 1000. Further away the score drops smoothly: about 720 points at 500 km, about 520 at 1,000 km and about 265 at 2,000 km. The How to play page has the full table.

What if two cities have the same name?

The one closest to your pin counts. If you are asked about "Springfield", any Springfield will do, so you are never punished for thinking of a different city with the same name. After you guess, the map shows every place with that name.

What does "better than X% of players" mean?

After the daily challenge you see what share of the other players that day scored lower than you. If you have the best score of the day it says 100%.

What do the coloured squares in the shared result mean?

Each square is one round: 🎯 is a perfect 1000, 🟩 is 750 or more, 🟨 is 400 or more, 🟧 is 100 or more and 🟥 is below 100. You can share your result without spoiling the cities for your friends.

Accounts and streaks

Do I need an account?

No. You can play every mode as a guest. An account, created with your email or with Google, lets you keep your best score and your streak across devices.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts how many days in a row you have finished the daily challenge on the day it came out. If you have not played yet today, your streak is still safe until midnight UTC. Playing an old day from the archive does not count towards the streak.

What is the difference between best score and daily best?

Your best score is the highest total you have ever reached in a game. Your daily best is the highest total you have reached today; it goes back to zero when a new day starts.

How do I delete my account?

Open the account menu in the top right corner and choose "Delete account". Your email, scores and daily results are removed straight away.

The map and the data

Where do the cities come from?

From GeoNames, an open database of places around the world. The game uses every city and town with at least 5,000 inhabitants, more than 64,000 in total. The About page explains how they are chosen for each round.

Why is a city spelled differently from what I expected?

Many places have several spellings, especially names written in another alphabet. The game uses the spelling shown on the map itself, so you can find it by zooming in. If you spot a name that looks wrong, please let us know.

A city seems to be in the wrong place. What can I do?

Send the city name and what looks wrong to contact@pinthecity.me. The data comes from open sources and occasionally a place is placed oddly; reports help fix it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. PinTheCity runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to install.
